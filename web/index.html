THE BOUNDARY

This is an educational experiment using IBM's public synthetic transactions. No employer or customer data is used. A model score is not evidence of financial crime.

02 / THE METHOD

Keep time moving
in one direction.

Features use only earlier outgoing payments in the sender's preceding 24 hours. Transactions at the same timestamp do not see one another. History continues across splits; labels never enter it.

TRAIN121,962Sep 1–6, 2022
VALIDATION38,688Sep 7–8, 2022
TEST40,731Sep 9–10, 2022

Fit on train only. Select the model by validation AP, then evaluate test. No refit on validation, test-driven tuning, oversampling or new ML algorithm.

Selected model: 100 Random Forest trees, maximum depth 12, minimum leaf size 10, balanced class weights and seed 42.

03 / THE INPUTS

One amount.
Six pieces of context.

The scope is deliberately narrow: positive ACH transfers with both currencies set to US Dollar. Histories cover that subset only—not every transaction an account made.

Inspect the seven features
log_amount
Natural log of 1 + the current USD amount.
sender_count_24h
Number of strictly earlier outgoing transfers.
sender_total_24h
Total USD value of those transfers.
sender_mean_24h
Their mean USD value.
sender_unique_receivers_24h
Number of distinct earlier receivers.
is_new_receiver_24h
1 if this receiver is absent from that history.
amount_to_prior_mean_ratio
Current amount / historical mean; 0 with no history.

WHAT NOT TO CONCLUDE

Synthetic-data results do not prove real-bank effectiveness. Accounts may repeat across splits, so this does not test unseen accounts. The short USD/ACH window omits incoming flows, graph structure and other currencies. The first day starts with no earlier history. An unusual payment is not necessarily a laundering payment.

NOT A VERDICT

These handcrafted scenarios are not Kaggle records and have no known laundering labels. The score is a ranking output—not a calibrated probability, approval or accusation.

TRANSACTION CONTEXT

USD · outgoing ACH · prior 24 hours

Use only earlier outgoing ACH/USD payments. With no history, the model treats the receiver as new and history features as zero. Illustrative inputs may be outside the training distribution; a score does not validate their realism.
